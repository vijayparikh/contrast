[Apple Music’s 404 page](https://music.apple.com/404) has a fully functional iPod simulator, and it’s surprisingly faithful to the original. Head into Settings > About, hold down the Select button for five seconds, and you’ll unlock a game of BrickOut (officially known as Brick by Apple).

The original iPod really was a little work of art. Simple, purpose-built, and brilliantly designed. You could do just about everything with your thumb without even having to look at the screen. There was something almost magical about that simplicity.

I would absolutely love to see Apple bring it back for a limited run. No redesigns, no unnecessary features, no attempt to reinvent the wheel. Just take the original iPod, replace the hard drive with an SSD, and leave everything else alone.

Honestly, the marketing campaign could be just one line: [“Shut up and take my money!”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kpw92fH0wdc)
